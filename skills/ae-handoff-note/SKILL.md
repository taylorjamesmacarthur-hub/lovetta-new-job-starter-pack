# AE handoff note

## 1. Qualify before you book

A meeting is only worth the AE's time if all four of these are true. If any is missing, say which one and suggest a question to ask the broker.

- **Right person:** a producer who owns the client relationships.
- **Real situation:** a renewal coming in hot, employees struggling with out-of-pocket costs, or a client asking for options. They don't need to name the client.
- **Why now:** a date, such as the renewal month or a planning meeting.
- **Something for the AE to answer:** one specific question the broker wants answered.

## 2. The note (under 120 words, Slack-ready)

```
New meeting: [Producer], [Firm] · [date/time]
Who: [role, book size or focus, how we met]
What they're seeing: [situation in their words]
Why now: [renewal month / trigger]
They want: [the one question]
Heads up: [history with us, sensitivities, or "none"]
```

Rules:
- Use their words for the situation, not your pitch.
- Never include client names unless the broker offered them.
- If they mentioned a past bad experience, it goes under Heads up, every time.

## 3. After the meeting

Ask the AE one question: "Was this worth your time? If not, what was missing?" Their answer tells you how to qualify next time.
