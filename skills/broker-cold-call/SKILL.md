# Broker cold call

A producer advises lots of employers and guards that book. They take your call when you give them a reason that makes them look good to their clients. Your goal on the call is a 15-minute meeting with a real situation behind it. You are not trying to close anything.

## 1. Build the opener (5 beats, under 30 seconds)

1. **Permission:** "Hi [name], it's Lovetta. I know I'm calling out of the blue. Got 30 seconds?"
2. **Role check:** "You work mostly with mid-sized employers, right?"
3. **Reason:** use the warmest reason you have, in this order:
   - A colleague: "[Colleague] on your team already works with us."
   - A client like theirs: one published story that fits their book.
   - Timing: "Renewals are coming in hot this year."
4. **Situation question:** "Of your renewals in the next six months, is anyone coming in higher than they expected?"
5. **Ask:** "Worth 15 minutes with my AE so you have one more option to bring them?"

Research cap: 5 minutes per producer. The conversation will tell you the rest.

## 2. The three brush-offs

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "No client comes to mind." | "Totally fair, I'm not asking for a name. When do you start planning your next renewals? Let's put 15 minutes down for then." Get a date, not a "someday". |
| "I had a bad experience with you guys." | Thank them, ask what happened, and listen to the whole story without defending. "Can someone come back to you once we've looked into it?" Then log it and tell your AE today. |
| "I already have a contact there." | "Perfect, I work with them. My job is to make sure the rest of your team gets the same access. Who else should be in that conversation?" Then tell your AE right away. |

## 3. Role-play mode

If the user says "role-play", play the producer. Be busy and a little skeptical, and use one brush-off from the table. After the call, give exactly three notes: what landed, what to cut, and the line to say next time.

## 4. After the call

Log it (use `log-activity`). If you booked a meeting, write the handoff note (use `ae-handoff-note`). If not, write down what they said, because it's data for the next call.
